--- webometrics/SOURCE_history.txt
Webometrics Ranking of World Universities - historical editions
Recovered from Wayback Machine (web.archive.org) captures of
webometrics.info. Retrieval date: 13 August 2026.

Method: for each target edition, the paginated world-ranking list
pages were fetched from Wayback captures taken inside that edition's
publication window (July editions: captures Aug 1 - Jan 27 next year;
January editions: captures Feb 1 - Jul 27). Pages parsed
programmatically (regex row extraction, pandas). Country column is
the ISO-2 code taken from the row's flag-image filename, uppercased.
A leading footnote marker of the form '(N) ' was stripped from
institution names where present. Ranks are competition ranking
(ties share a rank; the following rank is skipped), validated as:
first rank = 1, sorted ascending, rank <= position for every row.

Direct access to www.webometrics.info was not possible from this
machine (connection refused), hence Wayback-only recovery.
A GitHub search (api.github.com, 'webometrics' repos/code) found no
usable full mirrors of historical editions.

Edition-specific notes:
- 2004-12: old site top1000.asp (100 rows/offset page). Wayback has
  Dec 2004 captures of offsets 0-900 EXCEPT offset=200 (never
  captured in that state), so output truncates at the contiguous
  top 200. A Feb 2005 capture cluster of the same URLs shows clearly
  different data (top-200 name overlap only 44-80%), confirming the
  Dec 2004 and Feb 2005 states are distinct edition states; the Feb
  2005 cluster was fetched for comparison only (history_raw/
  cmp_2005-02/) and is not emitted as an edition.
- 2005-05: recovered from a static mirror the site itself hosted
  (top2000.asp.htm and top2000.asp-offset=N.htm, 100 rows/page),
  captured Oct-Nov 2005. Pages carry 'Last modified: 09/05/2005'.
  The live top2000.asp capture of 20 Jul 2005 is row-identical to the
  mirror's first page, so the data state was live by July 2005 and
  the stamp is read as 9 May 2005 (dd/mm); labeled 2005-05.
  Known site artifact: QUEEN MARY UNIVERSITY OF LONDON is listed
  twice (ranks 524 and 549) on the same source page; kept as-is.
- 2006-07: top3000.asp (50 rows/page); only offsets 0-300 were ever
  captured in the July-2006 window, so depth is top 350. No captures
  exist in a January-2006 window.
- 2009-01: January edition used because the July-2009 window has only
  6 non-contiguous offset captures (unusable beyond top 100).
- 2010-07: offsets 800/900/950 not captured in window; depth top 800.
- 2022-01: January edition used because July-2022 window lacks pages
  1,5,6,9 (contiguous depth would be only top 100). Page 9 (ranks
  901-1000) has no capture in the Jan-2022 window either; depth 900.
- 2024-07: page 9 not captured in window; depth top 900.
- Unrecoverable as full editions: 2004 beyond top 200, 2006 beyond
  top 350, July editions of 2009 and 2022 (January used instead).
- Where adjacent page captures weeks apart produced a one-row overlap
  at a page boundary (a single institution duplicated at ranks N/N+1:
  2016-07, 2018-07, 2020-07, 2021-07, 2022-01), the offending page
  was refetched from a capture taken close in time to its neighbour,
  which removed the artifact. No hand-editing of rows anywhere.
- 2022+ Wayback bodies are stored gzip-compressed; decompressed
  before parsing. Ties share a rank (competition ranking), which is
  why tied_positions > 0; ranks were NOT renumbered.
