Complete ARWU editions 2023-2025, retrieved 13 August 2026 by direct HTTP
client (code/00_fetch_shanghai_api.py) from the public ShanghaiRanking API:
  https://www.shanghairanking.com/api/pub/v1/arwu/rank?version={year}
1,000 rows per edition: exact ranks 1-100, bands 101-1000, alphabetical within
band as published. No transcription step involved; values are copied verbatim
from the JSON payload.

These SUPERSEDE the truncated arwu_{year}_webfetch.csv files described in
data/SOURCES.txt (those stop inside the 201-300 band, ~231 rows). To use:
copy into ~/uniranks/raw2/arwu/, delete the *_webfetch.csv files there, and
re-run the pipeline from 01_ingest.py (column layout already matches the
arwu_*.csv glob in that script).
